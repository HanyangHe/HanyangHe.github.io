    Research Projects

    Structure-Aware Methods Across Power-System Time Scales

    Learning useful models. Preserving their fidelity. Supporting reliable operating decisions.

    Slow-Time-Scale Forecasting & Energy Management

    Forecasting, resource scheduling, and operating decisions, primarily using aggregated energy models, algebraic constraints, and optimization.

    Fast Dynamic Modeling, Simulation & Control

    Physics-aware system identification, vector-field learning, numerical simulation, and dynamic control, primarily involving differential and differential-algebraic models.

    These two regimes share a common structure-aware research philosophy while addressing distinct engineering problems. When needed, fast dynamic analysis can inform or validate slow-time-scale operating decisions without requiring detailed transient models inside the energy-management optimization itself.
