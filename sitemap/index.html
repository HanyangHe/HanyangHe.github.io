    projects

    Adaptive Microgrid Digital Shadows

    Modular, physics-embedded identification with selective and causally validated updates when device dynamics change.

    Data-Efficient Load Forecasting

    Anisotropic random features and transfer learning for load forecasting with limited historical data and short input sequences.

    Dual-Port Grid-Forming Control of Modular Multilevel Converters

    Physics-based modeling and control redesign for dual-port grid-forming modular multilevel converters, validated in a back-to-back MMC Simulink system.

    Forecast-Adaptive Microgrid Energy Management

    Online PV forecast correction coupled with rolling energy dispatch and cone-relaxed network constraints.

    Frequency-Aware Islanded Microgrid Operation

    Coordinate frequency reserve, battery flexibility, EV charging, and load curtailment in renewable-rich islanded microgrids.

    Geometric Learning and Simulation of Dynamics

    Tangent-aware representations and geometry-aware time integration for learning differential equations on manifolds.

    Physics-Embedded MPC for Battery Charging

    Compact dictionary models that incorporate incomplete physics and support predictive control of a battery charging converter.

    Power Disturbance and Equipment Sensitivity

    Earlier research on voltage-disturbance response mechanisms, sensitive devices (e.g., AC contactors), and the consequences of power disturbances for sensitive industrial processes.
