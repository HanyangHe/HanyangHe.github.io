## Research Experience

### Pennsylvania State University | 2023–Present

**Research Assistant — Algorithms for Modern Power Systems (AMPS)**  
**Supervisors:** Yan Li, John Harlim, and Daning Huang.

**Fast dynamic modeling, simulation, and control**

- **Physics-embedded converter modeling:** Developed sparse dictionary models incorporating incomplete physical knowledge for model predictive control of battery charging converters.
- **Geometric learning and simulation:** Developed geometrically constrained sparse identification and contributed to kernel-based vector-field learning, with geometry-aware numerical integration for nonlinear dynamical systems.
- **Physics-embedded dictionary networks:** Developed PhDN to represent nested physical relationships through reusable intermediate expressions and differentiable parameter refinement.
- **LLM-Prior SINDy:** Developed LLM-guided physical-dictionary discovery and deterministic interaction expansion for data-efficient microgrid dynamic identification, online coefficient adaptation, and receding-horizon prediction.

**Slow-time-scale forecasting and energy management**

- **Data-efficient load forecasting:** Developed anisotropic orthogonal random-feature regression and transfer learning for short-term load forecasting with limited data and short input histories.
- **Forecast-adaptive microgrid EMS:** Developed rolling-horizon energy management integrating multi-modal PV forecast correction with second-order cone-relaxed power-flow constraints.
- **Frequency-aware islanded EMS:** Developed adaptive reserve allocation and service-aware battery, EV, and load coordination, assessing slow-time-scale operating decisions through fast frequency-response simulations.
